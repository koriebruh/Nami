> **Branch:** `feat/rum-part2` in `.worktrees/rum-part2/`.

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Finish the RUM sub-project with session replay: sampled rrweb recording in the browser with masking applied before anything leaves the page, chunked gzip upload, ClickHouse storage with its own retention, a replay player that cannot execute recorded content, a sessions list with a timeline of page views, errors and custom events beside the player, replay erasure, and the links between error events and replays. Part 2's merge also freezes the v1 SDK URLs.

**Architecture:** `nami-rum/` gains a second bundle, `nami-rum-replay.js` (`@rrweb/record`), which the v1 core loads with SRI only for replay-sampled sessions, and a third, internal one, the player (`rrweb-player`). The recorder masks at record time, cuts chunks every 10 s or 100 KiB, gzips them with `CompressionStream`, and posts each chunk to `POST /api/v1/rum/replay` on the engine's public listener as a header line plus the gzip bytes. The engine reuses Part 1's key, origin, per-IP and app checks, refuses replay for apps with replay off, checks the chunk with a streaming, bounded gunzip on a blocking thread, counts the daily byte cap, and stores the gzip bytes base64-encoded in `rum_replay_chunks`. The sessions API aggregates `rum_events` per session, joins replay chunk counts and Error tracking's `error_events` by `rum_session_id`, and streams replay chunks in pages. nami-ui serves the player as a standalone HTML document with its own nonce-only CSP, embedded by the session page; inside it, rrweb's replay iframe is sandboxed without `allow-scripts`.

**Tech Stack:** as Part 1. **No new crate** (`flate2` and `base64` are already dependencies). **New npm packages, exact pins:** in `nami-rum/`, `@rrweb/record@2.1.7` and `rrweb-player@2.1.7`, with `overrides` pinning `rrweb`, `rrweb-snapshot`, `rrdom`, `@rrweb/types`, `@rrweb/utils`, `@rrweb/replay` and `@rrweb/packer` to `2.1.7` (their own ranges are carets); in nami-ui, `@playwright/test@1.63.0` for the replay XSS browser test (R-RUM2-12). 2.2.0 of the rrweb packages was published on the day this plan was written, so the plan pins the previous release; a later version needs a ruling.

## Review Focus

- **A recorded page is hostile HTML.** Nothing in a chunk may run in Nami's origin: not a script, not an event-handler attribute, not a `javascript:` URL, not an `srcdoc` iframe, and the recorded styles cannot reach Nami's UI -> NAMI-RUM-19 (`tests/replay-xss.spec.ts`).
- **Masking happens in the visitor's browser.** Masked input values and masked text must never be in the uploaded bytes, checked on the gzip payload the recorder produces -> NAMI-RUM-16.
- **A 1 MiB chunk can claim to inflate to gigabytes.** The check streams, stops at 10 MiB plus one byte and never buffers the output -> NAMI-RUM-17.
- **A long session is up to 2,000 chunks of 1 MiB.** No read may collect a session into memory: chunk pages stay under the ClickHouse client's 32 MiB response cap and are streamed through the BFF -> NAMI-RUM-18.
- **The v1 SDK is frozen at this merge.** After it, the bytes behind `/rum/v1/*` never change -> NAMI-RUM-16.

## Decisions the spec leaves open (rulings)

- **R-RUM2-1 Chunk framing.** The body is one header line of JSON, `{"session_id", "seq", "ts_start", "ts_end", "sent_at"}` (32 hex; 0-1,999; client ms epochs; `deny_unknown_fields`; at most `MAX_CHUNK_HEADER_BYTES = 1024`), a `\n`, then the gzip bytes. `Content-Type: text/plain;charset=UTF-8` so no preflight (as R-RUM1-16). A missing newline, a bad header or an empty payload is a 400.
- **R-RUM2-2 Times.** `ts_start` and `ts_end` are stored corrected by the beacon skew rule (`received_at - clamp(sent_at - ts, 0, 1 h)`); a chunk with `ts_end < ts_start` or longer than `MAX_CHUNK_SPAN = 60 s` is a 400. The rrweb events inside keep the browser's clock; the player maps them to the timeline with the offset between the first stored `ts_start` and the first event's timestamp.
- **R-RUM2-3 Base64 storage.** Inserts are JSONEachRow, and a JSON string cannot carry raw bytes, so `data` holds the standard base64 of the gzip bytes exactly as sent (spec deviation), with `CODEC(ZSTD(3))`. A `bytes UInt32` column holds the gzip size, so listings never read `data`. Caps and usage count gzip bytes.
- **R-RUM2-4 When a chunk is cut.** At 10 s or when the serialized events reach 100 KiB uncompressed (`CHUNK_RAW_BYTES = 100 * 1024`), whichever is first. Chunks go out with a plain `fetch` (no keepalive, so no 64 KiB limit), one retry after 2 s. On page hide the pending chunk goes out with `fetch` keepalive when it is at most 60 KiB compressed, else it is dropped (a lost tail, documented). A single chunk over 1 MiB compressed stops recording for the rest of the session.
- **R-RUM2-5 Session caps.** The recorder stops 4 h after its first chunk (`MAX_REPLAY_DURATION_MS`) and after seq 1,999. The server refuses `seq >= MAX_CHUNKS_PER_SESSION = 2000` with 413 (the spec groups the session cap with the payload limits). A retried chunk is stored twice; reads keep one per `seq` (`LIMIT 1 BY seq`).
- **R-RUM2-6 Recorder options.** `maskAllInputs: true`; `maskInputOptions: {password: true}`; `maskTextClass: 'nami-mask'`; `maskTextSelector: '*'` when `replay_mask_all_text` (rrweb has no `maskAllText` option), else none; `blockClass: 'nami-block'`; `recordCanvas: false`; `recordCrossOriginIframes: false`; `inlineStylesheet: true`; `inlineImages: false`; `collectFonts: false`; no plugins (no console, no network); `sampling: {mousemove: 50, scroll: 150, input: 'last'}`; `checkoutEveryNms: 600000` (a full snapshot every 10 min, so one lost chunk breaks at most 10 min of replay). Option names are checked against the pinned `@rrweb/record` typings at phase start; a rename is adapted, a missing option stops the task.
- **R-RUM2-7 Replay sampling and loading.** At session start the core draws `replay = sampled && Math.random() < replay_sample_rate` and keeps it in the session state. Only then it adds `<script src="<core dir>/nami-rum-replay.js" integrity="<sri>" crossorigin="anonymous">`, where the core directory comes from its own `document.currentScript.src` and the SRI is baked into the core at build time (`emit.mjs` builds the replay bundle first). The replay bundle registers itself on `NamiRUM` and starts recording. `REPLAY_GZIP_BUDGET_BYTES = 64 * 1024`; the core stays within Part 1's 12 KiB.
- **R-RUM2-8 Freezing v1 (R-RUM1-15).** This phase's last commit switches `/rum/v1/*` to `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` and adds a test that pins the sha384 of `nami-rum.js` and `nami-rum-replay.js`. After the merge any change ships under `/rum/v2/`.
- **R-RUM2-9 Replay ingest limits.** The per-IP bucket of Part 1 is shared. A separate per-app bucket `REPLAY_CHUNKS_PER_SECOND = 50`, burst 100 (about 500 recorded sessions at once per app). `MAX_REPLAY_IN_FLIGHT = 16` places (16 x 1 MiB bodies); `MAX_REPLAY_CHECKS_IN_FLIGHT = 4` blocking decompression checks.
- **R-RUM2-10 What the check accepts.** The payload must gunzip within `MAX_REPLAY_DECOMPRESSED_BYTES = 10 MiB` (one byte more is a 413, read through a counting reader, never buffered) and parse as a JSON array (`serde_json::from_reader` into `IgnoredAny` over the streaming decoder) on `spawn_blocking`; anything else is a 400. The stored bytes are the bytes as sent.
- **R-RUM2-11 Player isolation without touching the app-wide CSP.** SvelteKit pages hydrate with inline scripts, so a nonce-only `script-src` on one page would need `kit.csp` for the whole app. Instead the player is a standalone HTML document returned by a `+server.ts` (`.../sessions/[sessionId=sessionid]/player`), which checks the session itself (endpoints do not run the `(app)` layout load), with: `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; script-src 'nonce-<random>'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; img-src https: data:; font-src https: data:; media-src https: data:; connect-src 'self'; base-uri 'none'; form-action 'none'; object-src 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'`, `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` (recorded images must not learn Nami's URLs), `Cache-Control: no-store`. Its only script is the player bundle served from `/rum/player/player.js` (public, no data, `nosniff`) with the nonce. rrweb's replay iframe inherits this CSP (it has no `src`) and keeps rrweb's default `sandbox="allow-same-origin"`; `UNSAFE_replayCanvas` (which adds `allow-scripts`) is never set. The session page embeds the document in an `<iframe>`; the two talk only through `postMessage` with `event.origin === location.origin` and `event.source` checked (`{type: "seek", ms}` down, `{type: "time", ms}` up).
- **R-RUM2-12 Browser test without an engine.** `tests/replay-xss.spec.ts` serves the player document from the same pure function the route uses (`playerDocument({nonce, chunksUrl})` in `#lib/rum/player.ts`) through `page.route`, answers the chunk request with crafted chunks, and asserts that no injected script ran (`window.__pwned` unset, no dialog, no request to the crafted `javascript:` or attacker URLs), that every replay iframe's `sandbox` lacks `allow-scripts`, and that a CSP violation was reported for the inline script.
- **R-RUM2-13 Chunk reads in pages.** `GET .../sessions/{session_id}/replay?from_seq=` streams NDJSON lines `{"seq", "ts_start", "ts_end", "data"}` for at most `REPLAY_PAGE_CHUNKS = 16` chunks (16 x 1 MiB x 4/3 base64 stays under `MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES`), then one line `{"next_seq": n | null}`. The BFF relays it as a stream. The player decodes base64, gunzips with `DecompressionStream` and feeds events to the replayer as pages arrive.
- **R-RUM2-14 Sessions list bounds.** Range at most 30 days (`MAX_SESSIONS_RANGE_DAYS`), `limit` 1-100 default 50, keyset cursor on `(started_at desc, session_id)`. The URL filter takes an exact path or a glob with `*` (Part 1's funnel matching). The timeline returns at most 5,000 RUM events and 500 errors (`truncated: true` beyond).
- **R-RUM2-15 Erasure of replays.** Each batch of Part 1's erasure loop (R-RUM1-22: up to `ERASE_BATCH_IDS` session ids read first and bound) deletes `rum_replay_chunks` with `session_id IN {ids:Array(String)}` and then the `rum_events` rows, so the bound list, not a subquery, decides what goes. App deletion deletes the app's chunks too.
- **R-RUM2-16 Links from errors.** The Error tracking issue page shows "View session" on an event that has both `rum_session_id` and `rum_app_id` tags; it links to the RUM session page, which answers 404 when the app or session is not in the project.

### Task 17 (NAMI-RUM-17): Replay chunk ingest and storage

**Covers:** R9.05  **Depends on:** NAMI-RUM-16 (wire format)
**Files:** create `src/rum/replay/{mod,store,routes}.rs`, `tests/rum/replay.rs`; modify `src/core/clickhouse.rs`, `src/core/router.rs`, `src/rum/mod.rs`.
**Data shape:**
```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS rum_replay_chunks (
    project_id UUID,
    app_id UUID,
    session_id String,
    seq UInt32,
    ts_start DateTime64(3, 'UTC'),
    ts_end DateTime64(3, 'UTC'),
    bytes UInt32,
    data String CODEC(ZSTD(3)),
    retention_days UInt16
) ENGINE = MergeTree
PARTITION BY toYYYYMMDD(ts_start)
ORDER BY (project_id, app_id, session_id, seq)
TTL toDateTime(ts_start) + toIntervalDay(retention_days)
```
`ChunkHeader` (R-RUM2-1), `ReplayChunkRow`; constants `MAX_CHUNK_GZIP_BYTES = 1 MiB`, `MAX_REPLAY_DECOMPRESSED_BYTES = 10 MiB`, `MAX_CHUNKS_PER_SESSION`, `MAX_CHUNK_SPAN`, `REPLAY_CHUNKS_PER_SECOND`, `REPLAY_BURST`, `MAX_REPLAY_IN_FLIGHT`, `MAX_REPLAY_CHECKS_IN_FLIGHT`.
**API (public listener):** `POST /api/v1/rum/replay?k=<key>` -> 202 `{"stored": true}`; 400 bad framing, header, gzip or JSON; 401 unknown key; 403 foreign origin or replay off (`replay_sample_rate = 0`); 413 body over 1 MiB, over 10 MiB decompressed, or `seq >= 2000`; 415 another content type or a `Content-Encoding`; 429 per IP, per app (replay bucket) or `daily_replay_bytes_cap` (`Retry-After` until UTC midnight); 503 no place. `OPTIONS` as R-RUM1-16.
**Behaviour:**
1. Checks in Part 1's order (key shape, per-IP, place, app, origin, replay on, replay bucket, DNT/GPC with `respect_dnt` (202 dropped), content type, body size, header, daily bytes cap), then the check of R-RUM2-10 under a check slot on `spawn_blocking`, then the insert with `INSERT_SETTINGS` and `retention_days = replay_retention_days`.
2. Usage counts `replay_chunks`, `replay_bytes` (gzip bytes) and `replay_rejected`.
3. The body is never logged; the client IP follows Part 1's rules.
**Tests (`tests/rum/replay.rs`):** `a_chunk_is_stored_as_sent_and_read_back_byte_for_byte`, `an_app_with_replay_off_gets_a_403`, `a_1_mib_chunk_is_accepted_and_one_byte_more_is_a_413`, `a_chunk_inflating_past_10_mib_is_a_413_without_buffering_it` (a gzip bomb fixture; the test also bounds peak memory through the check's counting reader), `seq_1999_is_accepted_and_2000_is_a_413`, `a_bad_header_missing_newline_or_non_json_payload_is_a_400`, `the_daily_replay_byte_cap_is_a_429`, `the_replay_bucket_is_separate_from_the_beacon_bucket`, `the_chunk_ttl_uses_replay_retention_days`, `a_foreign_origin_is_a_403`, plus a fuzz corpus of headers and payloads that never panics.
**Acceptance:** chunks round-trip; every status code above has a test.

### Task 18 (NAMI-RUM-18): Sessions and replay read API, replay erasure

**Covers:** R9.05, R9.09 (replay erasure)  **Depends on:** NAMI-RUM-17
